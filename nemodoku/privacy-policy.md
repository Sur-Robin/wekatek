# NemoDoku Privacy Policy

Last updated: 9 October 2026

This privacy policy covers NemoDoku, an Android puzzle game published by wekatek (package name `nz.co.wekatek.nemodoku`).

## Summary

NemoDoku does not collect, transmit or share any personal data. It has no accounts, no advertising and no analytics, and it does not request permission to use the internet.

## Data the app collects

None. NemoDoku does not access or collect personal or sensitive information such as your name, email address, contacts, location, photos, or device and advertising identifiers.

## Data stored on your device

NemoDoku saves your game settings on your device so they are still there the next time you open the app. This information does not identify you and is never sent to wekatek.

## Data sharing

NemoDoku shares no data with wekatek or with any third party. The app contains no advertising, analytics or other third-party tracking software.

## Device backup

If you have turned on Android's backup feature, Android may include NemoDoku's saved settings in the backup of your device that it keeps in your own Google account. That backup is controlled by you and handled by Google under Google's privacy policy. wekatek has no access to it.

## Retention and deletion

Because wekatek receives no data, it holds nothing to retain or delete. You can delete everything NemoDoku has saved on your device at any time by clearing the app's storage in Android's settings or by uninstalling the app.

## Children

NemoDoku is suitable for all ages and does not collect data from anyone, including children.

## Google Play

If you install NemoDoku from Google Play, Google may collect information about the download and about how the app performs, such as crash reports, under Google's own privacy policy. NemoDoku itself sends Google nothing.

## Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new "Last updated" date. The history of changes is public in the [wekatek repository](https://github.com/Sur-Robin/wekatek).

## Contact

For questions about this policy or about privacy in NemoDoku, open an issue at <https://github.com/Sur-Robin/wekatek/issues>.
